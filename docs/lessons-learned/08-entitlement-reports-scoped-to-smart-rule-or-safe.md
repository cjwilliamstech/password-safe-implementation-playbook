# Lesson: Entitlement/Access Reports Are Scoped to a Smart Rule or Safe, Not a Single Account

You can't pull a clean "who has access to this one account" report directly — reports
are scoped to a Smart Rule or a Safe. If your Smart Rules are broad or messy, your
reports inherit that mess.

**Takeaway:** treat Smart Rule architecture as a reporting-design decision, not just an
onboarding convenience — the granularity you choose when building Smart Rules directly
determines how clean your future entitlement reports will be.
